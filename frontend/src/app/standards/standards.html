Standards

Information about the plumbing standards and compliance documents used by the assistant.

Compliance Knowledge Base

The assistant searches plumbing compliance documents included in the application's knowledge base.

When you ask a question, relevant sections are retrieved first and then used to generate a contextual answer.

Supporting References

Where available, responses can include supporting references such as document names, relevant sections and page-level source information.

These references help users verify important compliance requirements against the original material.
